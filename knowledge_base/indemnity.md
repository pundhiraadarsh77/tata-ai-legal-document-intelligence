# INDEMNITY KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: General Indemnification

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
The contract should clearly identify which party is responsible for specified third-party claims, losses or damages arising from the agreed circumstances.

---

TITLE: Intellectual Property Indemnity

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Where relevant, the supplier may be responsible for third-party claims alleging that supplied products or services infringe intellectual property rights. The scope of protection should be clearly defined.

---

TITLE: Data Protection Indemnity

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Where appropriate, the parties may allocate responsibility for losses arising from data protection violations or security incidents. The applicable circumstances should be expressly stated.

---

TITLE: Indemnity for Regulatory Violations

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement may require a party to cover specified losses resulting from its failure to comply with applicable laws or regulatory obligations.

---

TITLE: Indemnity for Third-Party Claims

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
The indemnifying party's responsibilities for third-party claims should be clearly described. The contract should also address notice, cooperation and control of the claim where applicable.

---

TITLE: Indemnity Claim Procedure

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
The contract should specify how an indemnity claim must be notified and what information must be provided. Failure to follow the agreed procedure should be addressed clearly.

---

TITLE: Indemnity and Settlement

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should define who controls the defense and settlement of an indemnified claim. Settlements should generally follow the contractual requirements agreed by the parties.

---

TITLE: Indemnity Limitations

CATEGORY: INDEMNITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The contract should clarify whether indemnity obligations are subject to liability caps or specific exclusions. Any exceptions should be expressly stated to avoid uncertainty.
