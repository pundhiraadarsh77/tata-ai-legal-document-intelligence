# DISPUTE RESOLUTION KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Negotiation Before Dispute

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
The parties may be required to attempt good-faith negotiation before starting formal dispute proceedings. The contract should define the applicable process and time period.

---

TITLE: Mediation Process

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement may require the parties to attempt mediation before arbitration or litigation. The mediation process and applicable institution or rules should be clearly identified where relevant.

---

TITLE: Arbitration Agreement

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Where arbitration is selected, the contract should identify the applicable arbitration rules and process. The parties should clearly state their agreement to resolve covered disputes through arbitration.

---

TITLE: Arbitration Seat

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The contract should specify the legal seat of arbitration when arbitration is used. The selected seat can affect the procedural framework applicable to the arbitration.

---

TITLE: Arbitration Language

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
For international agreements, the parties should identify the language to be used during dispute proceedings where necessary. This can reduce uncertainty during arbitration or other formal proceedings.

---

TITLE: Litigation and Court Proceedings

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
If disputes are resolved through courts, the agreement should identify the applicable jurisdiction and court forum. The provision should be consistent with the governing law clause.

---

TITLE: Urgent Injunctive Relief

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The contract may allow a party to seek urgent court relief when immediate protection is required. The provision should clarify whether such relief can be sought despite another agreed dispute-resolution process.

---

TITLE: Dispute Escalation Procedure

CATEGORY: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DR-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement may establish an escalation process where unresolved issues are first referred to designated representatives or senior management before formal proceedings begin.
