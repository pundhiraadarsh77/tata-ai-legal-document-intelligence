# COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08

---

TITLE: Compliance With Applicable Laws

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
Each party should comply with laws and regulations applicable to its contractual activities. Relevant compliance responsibilities should be clearly allocated in the agreement.

---

TITLE: Supplier Code of Conduct

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Suppliers may be required to follow applicable supplier codes of conduct covering ethical business practices, labor standards and responsible operations.

---

TITLE: Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Contracts should prohibit bribery and other improper payments connected with the contractual relationship. Parties should comply with applicable anti-corruption requirements.

---

TITLE: Employment and Labor Compliance

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Suppliers should comply with applicable employment, workplace safety and labor requirements. Contractual responsibilities should be clearly defined where workforce obligations are relevant.

---

TITLE: Environmental Compliance

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
Where relevant to the services or products, suppliers should comply with applicable environmental laws and contractual sustainability requirements.

---

TITLE: Export Control and Trade Compliance

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
International transactions should consider applicable export controls, sanctions and trade restrictions. The parties should identify responsibilities for required compliance checks and documentation.

---

TITLE: Regulatory Reporting Obligations

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Where regulations require reporting or disclosures, the responsible party should provide the required information within the applicable timeframe.


TITLE: Compliance Records

CATEGORY: COMPLIANCE OBLIGATIONS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-COMP-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Relevant compliance records should be maintained for the period required by the contract or applicable law. Records should be available for review where appropriate.
