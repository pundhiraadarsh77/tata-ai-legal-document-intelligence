# LIABILITY KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Limitation of Liability

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
Contracts should clearly define whether either party's liability is limited and identify the applicable monetary cap or calculation method. The limitation should be reviewed against the nature and value of the agreement.

---

TITLE: Liability Cap Based on Contract Value

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
A liability cap may be linked to fees paid or payable under the agreement. The contract should clearly state the period and payment amount used to calculate the cap to avoid uncertainty.

---

TITLE: Exclusions from Liability Cap

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Certain liabilities may be excluded from a general liability cap depending on the contract and applicable law. These may include fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breaches, intellectual property infringement or other specifically identified risks.

---

TITLE: Indirect and Consequential Loss

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Contracts may address indirect, incidental, special or consequential losses separately from direct losses. The wording should clearly identify which categories of loss are excluded or included.

---

TITLE: Liability for Data Protection Breach

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
Where a contract involves personal or sensitive information, the parties should determine how liability for data protection incidents is allocated. Any special cap or uncapped liability should be expressly stated.

---

TITLE: Third-Party Claims

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should specify how liability is handled when a third party brings a claim connected with the services or products. Responsibilities for notice, defense and settlement should be clearly allocated where applicable.

---

TITLE: Liability for Regulatory Non-Compliance

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The parties should clearly allocate responsibility for losses arising from failure to comply with applicable laws or contractual compliance requirements. Any applicable liability limitations should be reviewed against the underlying regulatory risk.

---

TITLE: Aggregate Liability Period

CATEGORY: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-LIAB-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Where an aggregate liability cap applies, the contract should clarify the period over which claims are aggregated and whether separate caps apply to specific categories of liability.
