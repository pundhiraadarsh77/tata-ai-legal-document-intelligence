# AUDIT RIGHTS KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Supplier Audit Rights

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
The agreement may allow the customer to review supplier records and processes relevant to contractual obligations. The scope and conditions of the audit should be clearly defined.

---

TITLE: Audit Notice Requirements

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Contracts should specify how much advance notice is required before an audit. Exceptions may apply where immediate review is necessary due to serious compliance concerns.

---

TITLE: Access to Relevant Records

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Audit provisions should identify which records, systems or documents may be reviewed. Access should remain limited to information relevant to the contractual purpose.

---

TITLE: Confidentiality During Audit

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Information obtained during an audit should be handled confidentially. The parties should apply appropriate safeguards to protect sensitive business information.

---

TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Audit

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
Where regulatory compliance is important, the contract may provide audit rights to verify compliance with applicable requirements. The audit scope should reflect the relevant obligations.

---

TITLE: Third-Party Audit Reports

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
The agreement may permit recognized independent audit reports or certifications to satisfy certain audit requirements. The accepted standards and reporting period should be clearly identified.

---

TITLE: Audit Findings and Remediation

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The contract should define how audit findings are communicated and how identified issues are corrected. Where appropriate, remediation timelines should be agreed between the parties.

---

TITLE: Audit Costs and Frequency

CATEGORY: AUDIT RIGHTS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-AUD-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should clarify who bears audit costs and how frequently audits may be conducted. Excessive or unnecessary audits may be limited through contractual conditions.
