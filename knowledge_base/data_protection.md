# DATA PROTECTION KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Personal Data Handling

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
Personal data should be collected, used and stored only for legitimate contractual or business purposes. Access should be limited to authorized personnel.

---

TITLE: Data Processing Responsibilities

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Contracts should clearly identify the responsibilities of each party when personal data is processed. The required processing activities and permitted uses should be defined.

---

TITLE: Data Security Controls

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained to protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

---

TITLE: Data Breach Notification

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should define how suspected or confirmed data breaches are reported. Notification timelines and required incident information should be clearly specified where applicable.

---

TITLE: Cross-Border Data Transfer

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
Contracts involving international data transfers should address applicable transfer requirements and safeguards. The parties should identify responsibilities for maintaining lawful data transfers.

---

TITLE: Data Retention and Deletion

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Personal data should not be retained longer than required for the agreed purpose or applicable legal obligations. Contracts should define deletion or return requirements where appropriate.

---

TITLE: Sub-Processor Data Access

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
A supplier should obtain appropriate authorization before allowing subcontractors or other third parties to process personal data. Sub-processors should follow applicable contractual data protection requirements.

---

TITLE: Data Subject Requests

CATEGORY: DATA PROTECTION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-DP-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Where applicable, contracts should define how requests relating to personal data are handled. The parties should cooperate in providing information or assistance required under applicable data protection obligations.
