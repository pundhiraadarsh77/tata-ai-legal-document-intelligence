# CONFIDENTIALITY KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTERIES - 08


TITLE: Supplier Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Standard

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
Suppliers must protect confidential business information received from Tata and use it only for the agreed contractual purpose. Access should be limited to authorized personnel, and reasonable security controls should be maintained.

---

TITLE: Customer Information Protection

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Customer information received during a business relationship should be treated as confidential. Suppliers should prevent unauthorized access or disclosure and should use customer information only for legitimate contractual activities.

---

TITLE: Protection of Technical Information

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Technical specifications, designs, source code, system architecture and other proprietary technical information should not be disclosed to unauthorized parties. Suppliers should apply appropriate access controls and security measures.

---

TITLE: Confidential Pricing and Commercial Information

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Pricing, discounts, commercial terms, business strategies and negotiation information should be treated as confidential. Such information should not be shared with competitors or other unauthorized third parties without contractual permission.

---

TITLE: Third-Party Disclosure of Confidential Information

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
A supplier should not disclose Tata's confidential information to subcontractors or other third parties unless the disclosure is authorized. Approved third parties should be subject to confidentiality obligations that provide appropriate protection.

---

TITLE: Confidentiality After Contract Termination

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-006

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Confidentiality obligations should normally continue after the agreement expires or is terminated for the period specified in the contract. Information that remains confidential by its nature should continue to receive appropriate protection.

---

TITLE: Security Incident and Unauthorized Disclosure

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-007

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Unauthorized access, loss, disclosure or misuse of confidential information should be reported to the designated legal or compliance contact without undue delay. The supplier should provide relevant information about the incident and cooperate with required remediation.

---

TITLE: Return or Destruction of Confidential Information

CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY

REFERENCE_ID: POL-CONF-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
When a contractual relationship ends, confidential information should be returned or securely destroyed when requested, subject to applicable legal or regulatory retention requirements. Any retained information should continue to be protected.
