# PAYMENT TERMS KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Standard Supplier Payment Period

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
Supplier invoices should normally be paid within the payment period specified in the agreement. The contract should clearly state when the payment period begins and what documents or conditions are required before payment becomes due.

---

TITLE: Invoice Submission Requirements

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Contracts should define the required invoice format, submission method and supporting documents. Invoices that do not contain required information may be returned for correction, subject to the agreed contractual process.

---

TITLE: Payment Linked to Acceptance

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Where payment is linked to acceptance of goods or services, the contract should clearly define the acceptance criteria and review period. Payment conditions should not rely on undefined or subjective acceptance requirements.

---

TITLE: Late Payment and Interest

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Any interest or other consequence for late payment should be expressly stated in the contract and should comply with applicable law. The agreement should specify when late payment is considered to have occurred.

---

TITLE: Milestone-Based Payments

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
For projects involving multiple deliverables, payments may be linked to defined milestones. Each milestone should have measurable completion criteria, an agreed payment amount and a clear process for confirming completion.

---

TITLE: Taxes and Withholding

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
The contract should identify how applicable taxes, duties and withholding requirements affect payments. Responsibilities for tax documentation and compliance should be clearly allocated between the contracting parties.

---

TITLE: Disputed Invoice Procedure

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should provide a process for raising and resolving invoice disputes. A party disputing an invoice should identify the disputed amount and reason within the contractual period, while undisputed amounts should be handled according to the agreed payment terms.

---

TITLE: Currency and Payment Method

CATEGORY: PAYMENT TERMS

REFERENCE_ID: POL-PAY-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Contracts involving international suppliers should specify the payment currency, permitted payment method and responsibility for transaction or banking charges. Any foreign exchange mechanism should be clearly defined where applicable.
