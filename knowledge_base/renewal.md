# RENEWAL KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Automatic Contract Renewal

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
The contract should clearly state whether it renews automatically after the initial term. The renewal period and applicable conditions should also be specified.

---

TITLE: Renewal Notice Period

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Where advance notice is required to prevent renewal, the contract should clearly specify the notice period and permitted method of notice.

---

TITLE: Renewal Term

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should identify the duration of each renewal period. Different renewal periods should be clearly distinguished from the initial contract term.

---

TITLE: Renewal Pricing

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Contracts should explain whether pricing changes during renewal and how any adjustment is calculated. Undefined renewal price changes may create uncertainty.

---

TITLE: Renewal Subject to Performance

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
Renewal may be made conditional on satisfactory performance or compliance with specified requirements. The relevant conditions should be measurable and clearly stated.

---

TITLE: Renewal and Updated Terms

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should clarify whether contractual terms automatically continue during renewal or require updated written terms. Any changes should be documented clearly.

---

TITLE: Non-Renewal Procedure

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The contract should define how a party can provide notice of non-renewal. The notice should follow the required timing and communication method.

---

TITLE: Renewal and Regulatory Changes

CATEGORY: RENEWAL

REFERENCE_ID: POL-REN-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Where applicable, renewal should consider changes in laws or regulatory requirements that may affect continued performance. The parties may need to review affected contractual obligations before renewal.
