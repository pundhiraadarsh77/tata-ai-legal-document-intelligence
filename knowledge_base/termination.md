# TERMINATION KNOWLEDGE BASE
# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Termination for Convenience

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
A contract may allow either party to terminate the agreement for convenience by providing a specified notice period. The notice requirement and any payment obligations arising from termination should be clearly stated.

---

TITLE: Termination for Material Breach

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should identify circumstances that constitute a material breach and provide a process for issuing notice and allowing a reasonable cure period where appropriate. Termination rights should follow the contractual procedure.

---

TITLE: Immediate Termination Events

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Certain events may permit immediate termination, such as serious confidentiality violations, unlawful conduct, insolvency or significant compliance failures. The contract should clearly identify the applicable events.

---

TITLE: Termination Notice Requirements

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Termination notices should follow the method and notice period specified in the agreement. The contract should identify the permitted communication method and when a notice is considered received.

---

TITLE: Effect of Termination

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should specify what happens after termination, including outstanding payments, return of property, completion of transition activities and continuation of obligations that are intended to survive termination.

---

TITLE: Transition and Handover Obligations

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Where services are operationally important, the supplier may be required to support an orderly transition after termination. Handover requirements, access to information and transition assistance should be defined in the contract.

---

TITLE: Termination Due to Regulatory Change

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
A contract may provide termination or renegotiation rights when changes in applicable law make continued performance unlawful or commercially impractical. The agreement should define the process for addressing such changes.

---

TITLE: Termination and Record Retention

CATEGORY: TERMINATION

REFERENCE_ID: POL-TERM-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
Termination should not automatically remove legal or regulatory record-retention obligations. The parties should identify which records must be retained and how confidential information will continue to be protected after termination.
