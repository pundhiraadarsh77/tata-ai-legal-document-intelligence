# TOTAL ENTRIES - 08


TITLE: Governing Law Clause

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-001

JURISDICTION: Global including India, UK, Australia and Norway

GUIDANCE:
The contract should clearly identify the law that will govern its interpretation and enforcement. The selected jurisdiction should be expressly stated.

---

TITLE: Jurisdiction of Courts

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-002

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The agreement should specify which courts or legal forum will have jurisdiction over disputes where applicable. The clause should avoid unclear or conflicting jurisdiction requirements.

---

TITLE: Exclusive Jurisdiction

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-003

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Where exclusive jurisdiction is intended, the contract should clearly state the courts that have exclusive authority to resolve disputes.

---

TITLE: Non-Exclusive Jurisdiction

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-004

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
A non-exclusive jurisdiction clause allows disputes to be brought before specified courts without necessarily preventing proceedings elsewhere where legally permitted.

---

TITLE: Conflict of Laws

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-005

JURISDICTION: Global including UK and Australia

GUIDANCE:
Contracts involving multiple countries should address which governing law applies when different legal systems could otherwise create uncertainty.

---

TITLE: Local Law Compliance

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-006

JURISDICTION: Global including India

GUIDANCE:
Selecting a governing law does not remove obligations imposed by mandatory applicable laws. Parties should continue to comply with relevant local regulatory requirements.

---

TITLE: Change in Applicable Law

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-007

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The contract may specify how changes in applicable law affect the parties' obligations. Significant legal changes may require review or modification of contractual provisions.

---

TITLE: Governing Law and Contract Interpretation

CATEGORY: GOVERNING LAW

REFERENCE_ID: POL-GL-008

JURISDICTION: Global

GUIDANCE:
The governing law clause should be consistent with other dispute-resolution provisions in the agreement. Conflicting clauses may create uncertainty about how disputes should be handled.
